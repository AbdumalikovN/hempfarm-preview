An operating cluster, not a project on paper

Hemp Farm is a major investment project of the Republic of Uzbekistan: the Cabinet of Ministers monitors the fulfilment of its investment commitments every month. The infrastructure has been built, the licence has been obtained, and exports have been under way since 2025. We offer investors not an idea but an operating site with a clear path to growth.

Government status

The agro-industrial cluster was established by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 511 of 20 September 2022, and the project has the status of a major investment project. Its operations are licensed (licence No. 108836).

Infrastructure built

Water supply of 63,000 m³, four Valley center-pivot irrigation systems, a 1,000 kW transformer and a Sunrise greenhouse. The decortication line is operating; three deep-processing lines are contracted and paid for, with launch scheduled for Q4 2026.

Market confirmed

2025: first export shipment (China); 2026: export contract with a buyer in India. Target markets: Asia, Türkiye and the EU, the Persian Gulf, North America and the CIS.
