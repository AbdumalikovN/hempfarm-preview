Two product lines from one stalk: textiles and composites

Bast fiber in three refinement grades for the textile industry, and clean hurd with finished products made from it for construction and logistics. Every batch comes with a quality certificate and export documents.

Fiber Hurd & pallets Specifications Terms

Textile line · bast fiber

  • Primary fiber → degummed → cottonin (staple length 30–50 mm)
  • Blending with cotton on standard spinning equipment
  • End product of this line: cottonized fiber
80%

Composites line · hurd

  • Clean, low-dust hurd as raw material
  • End product: molded pallets (2 × 1,600 t line)
  • Hurd as raw material: hempcrete, insulation, animal bedding; board materials under development

The 20/80 ratio is approximate: in agriculture, yield depends on variety and moisture and varies by a few percent.

Hurd · 80% of the stalk

A valuable product, not waste

Clean, uniform, low-dust hurd: the 80% of the stalk that most producers throw away. For us, it is a product line in its own right and the raw material for our own products.

01

Molded pallets — the end product of the composites line

Pressing line: 2 × 1,600 t. Pallet: 1,200 × 800 × 130 mm, about 15.6 kg; stackable design, exempt from ISPM-15 fumigation and marking. Production from Q4 2026.

02

Board materials — under development

The next product in the composites line: hurd boards for furniture and construction.

03

Hurd as raw material

Hempcrete and green building, acoustic and thermal insulation, animal bedding, bioenergy. Supplied in bulk or in big bags; particle size and moisture content are measured and confirmed for every batch.
