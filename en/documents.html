The licence is open for verification; test reports, official records and sample export documents are provided on request — leave your name, company and email, and the foreign trade department will reply within 1–2 business days.

LICENCE
Valid · open for verification

Licence No. 108836

Ministry of Agriculture of the Republic of Uzbekistan · licence register No. L-53247 · TIN 308739544 · valid from 15 November 2022 to 15 November 2027. Seven types of activity: cultivation, import and export, processing, storage, sale, receipt and transportation of industrial hemp with a THC content of up to 0.2% for industrial purposes.

The licence carries a QR code for verification in the register
Legal basis

Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 511

Dated 20 September 2022, “On measures to organize an agro-industrial cluster in Navoi Region”. The project has the status of a major investment project: the Cabinet of Ministers monitors the fulfilment of its investment obligations every month.

The resolution was implemented through decision No. 194-5-0-Q/22 of the Navoi Region khokimiyat dated 28 September 2022: 1,015 ha in Kiziltepa District were allocated for the cluster.

Regulatory acts of government bodies are not published on the website as files.

Verification in the state register

The electronic licence has a QR code: scan it to open the HEMPFARM JV entry in the Ministry of Agriculture licence register.

✦ Industrial hemp varieties with a THC content below 0.2% are grown exclusively for industrial purposes. The activity is not related to the production of narcotic or psychotropic substances.
