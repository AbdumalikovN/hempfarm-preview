Industrial hemp is not a drug

It is an agricultural crop grown for fiber and building materials. Here we explain how it differs from drug varieties, how the state controls it and what is made from it.

THC below 0.2% Licence No. 108836 State crop inspection
In brief

Four facts about industrial hemp

Industrial hemp and drug-type cannabis are different varieties of the same species, Cannabis sativa. They are grown for different purposes and differ above all in their THC content.

< 0,2%

THC in our crops

THC is the substance responsible for the psychoactive effect. At this level, there is no such effect.

THC content

Where the line is drawn

Varieties with a very low THC content are classed as industrial hemp. The limit is set by law: 0.3% in the EU, the US and Canada. Hemp Farm's crops are below 0.2%.

State oversight

How every harvest is checked

  1. LicenceCultivation is carried out under Ministry of Agriculture licence No. 108836. The cluster was established by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 511 of 20 September 2022.
  2. Low-THC varietiesOnly industrial varieties of Cannabis sativa with a THC content below 0.2% are sown.
  3. Sampling before harvestThe competent authorities take samples in the field, a laboratory confirms the THC level, and an official report is drawn up.
  4. Security and registrationThe cluster's territory is guarded by the National Guard, and the crops are entered in the cadastral register.
  5. Export documentsEvery batch is accompanied by an electronic phytosanitary certificate with a QR code.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about industrial hemp

Can drugs be made from industrial hemp? +
No. In industrial varieties, the content of THC, the substance responsible for the psychoactive effect, is below 0.2%. That is tens of times lower than in drug-type varieties, so industrial hemp has no psychoactive effect.
Is it legal to grow industrial hemp in Uzbekistan? +
Yes, under a state licence. The Hemp Farm cluster was established by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 511 of 20 September 2022, and cultivation is carried out under Ministry of Agriculture licence No. 108836.
Who checks the harvest? +
Before harvest, the competent authorities take samples in the field. A laboratory confirms that the THC content is below 0.2%, after which an official report is drawn up. The cluster's territory is under guard.
Which part of the plant does Hemp Farm use? +
The stalk. Decortication lines turn it into bast fiber for textiles and hurd, the woody part of the stalk, for building materials and molded pallets.
Where else is industrial hemp grown? +
Industrial hemp is grown legally in many countries, including EU member states, the US, Canada and China. In the EU, the US and Canada, the permitted THC limit for industrial varieties is 0.3%.
